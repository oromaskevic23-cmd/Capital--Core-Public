Use this crowned globe identity for IMPERIAL Core. The older crowned letter I wrapped in a ribbon is superseded for current public presentation. Do not substitute it or invent a new symbol without the owner's instruction.

## Presentation

- Display the emblem on its transparent background, without square backplates, hard rectangular borders or cropping.
- Use the complete supplied panorama at the beginning of the public website. Blend its lower edge gradually into a dark cosmic background; maintain the original image proportions.
- Prefer gold, deep blue and near-black. Keep sufficient contrast and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Preserve the spelling **IMPERIAL Core** and public signature **RomaskevicH**.
- Artwork illustrates a long-term vision. Numbers, fictional buildings and scenes are not evidence of deployed agent capacity, customers, offices or production services.
