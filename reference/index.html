GLOBAL VISION · LOCAL INTELLIGENCE

Capital Core · Enterprise

The target: a dedicated team of universal Nano Core Agents for every major city in the world. Local knowledge, professional capabilities and shared intelligence within the Federated Multi-Orchestrator Architecture.

Architecture roadmap · City teams are planned; active global coverage is not verified.

01

Every city. Its own context.

City teams develop an understanding of local markets, languages, industries and business opportunities, with dated sources and clear evidence.

02

Universal teams. Defined capabilities.

Dynamic NCA teams combine research, business analysis, software engineering and operations. Skills and permissions are assigned for each mission; universality does not grant unrestricted authority.

03

ORION · collective intelligence

ORION connects knowledge from city teams, compares findings and prepares recommendations. It supports shared learning across the network while domain orchestrators remain responsible for authorized execution.

04

Federated execution

Domain orchestrators coordinate missions across organizations and professional teams within Runtime Domains. AI Passport, Enterprise IMPERIAL Skills, Guardian Core, Approval Gateway and Audit Ledger preserve accountability.

Capital Core supplies economic and business intelligence. Financial analysis does not itself authorize transactions; CRYPTO IMPERIAL Ecosystem retains its separate boundary.
