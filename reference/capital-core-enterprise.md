# Capital Core — Enterprise city-team architecture

**Status: SPECIFIED / TARGET ARCHITECTURE**  
**Direction recorded: 28 October 2026**  
**Architect: Alexander Romaskevich — RomaskevicH**

Capital Core remains the economic and business-intelligence component of IMPERIAL Core. “Enterprise” describes the expanded organizational scope; it does not rename the canonical component.

## Vision

The target is a dedicated team of universal Nano Core Agents for every major city worldwide. This is a long-term coverage objective. The public website does not establish an active city fleet, a deployed global service or a verified number of operating agents.

A city is a context for local expertise, not automatically an office, legal entity or physical datacenter. Multiple organizations and professional teams may work in the same city. Team size changes with the mission and validated capacity.

## Universal teams

Universality belongs to the team's range of professional capabilities. Individual NCA retain a defined role, skill set, authorization scope and Runtime Domain. A team can combine research, market analysis, business planning, software engineering, operations and specialist review without granting every member unrestricted access.

| Area | Intended output |
| --- | --- |
| Local market intelligence | Dated research on industries, language, demand and business conditions |
| Opportunity assessment | Source-backed hypotheses and explicit uncertainty |
| Enterprise planning | Bounded business and operational proposals |
| Engineering support | Scoped technical work with verification evidence |
| Local coordination | Mission context shared with authorized teams and domain orchestrators |
| Evidence review | Findings linked to sources, revision dates and observed outcomes |

## ORION — collective intelligence

The Architect selected ORION's collective-intelligence role for this city network.

ORION aggregates authorized knowledge from city teams, compares findings, identifies contradictions and prepares recommendations. It supports shared learning between cities while respecting the Public / Private Boundary. Shared insights do not imply unrestricted access to raw private records.

ORION does not become a single global execution orchestrator. Recommendations do not grant permissions, approve transactions or override the Architect. Domain orchestrators remain responsible for authorized execution.

## Federation and authority

The existing Federated Multi-Orchestrator Architecture is preserved:

- The Architect retains final architectural authority.
- HANTER remains the AI Command Center and deputy to the Architect.
- Multiple domain orchestrators coordinate bounded missions across organizations and teams.
- Nano Core Agents execute only within their capabilities and authorized Runtime Domains.
- AI Passport, Enterprise IMPERIAL Skills, Guardian Core, Approval Gateway, Audit Ledger and Zero Trust remain architectural requirements.
- Public / Private Boundary and CRYPTO IMPERIAL Ecosystem Boundary remain separate and explicit.

Capital Core provides economic intelligence. Financial intelligence is distinct from transaction authority and execution. Any economic execution must pass through the applicable authorization and audit boundaries; analytical confidence does not grant payment authority.

## Evidence-led expansion

| Stage | Evidence needed before expanding |
| --- | --- |
| Select a pilot city | Defined mission, local context and source availability |
| Form a bounded team | Identified capabilities, responsibilities and authorized scope |
| Validate useful work | Reproducible deliverables and documented verification |
| Connect shared knowledge | Authorized information exchange and source traceability |
| Expand to more cities | Demonstrated value, capacity and accountable operation |

The global objective does not imply that these stages have been completed. Active-city counts, production readiness and business outcomes require dated evidence before publication as facts.
